title: Documentation
summary: The application guide, from the first agenda to the signed minutes.
---

This documentation accompanies the municipal council management application. It
follows the real flow of a meeting: **prepare** the matters in **Documents** and
the agenda in **Meetings**, **conduct** the meeting in meeting mode, then
**establish** the minutes and the register of decisions.

<Callout kind="tip">
  The documentation is available at any time from the navigation, without leaving your current
  work. The search box (top right) covers every page.
</Callout>
